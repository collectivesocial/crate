## Why Chosen

Work item #1: **Initial lexicon suite**. Simon is the Lexicon Designer and owns the ATProto schema contract for the entire project. Lexicons are the foundation — everything else (API endpoints, web components, importers) depends on them.

## Work Summary

Authored 9 `social.crate.*` lexicons in `lexicons/social/crate/`:

1. `rss.feed` — subscribed RSS/Atom feed with configurable destination
2. `podcast.episode` — individual podcast episode (typically imported)
3. `making.project` — unified making/build record (fiber, code, site, garden, illustration-set, other)
4. `making.update` — progress update or journal entry for projects
5. `talk` — conference talk or presentation
6. `illustration` — stick-figure or artwork with alt text
7. `note` — Zettelkasten-style PKM note (backbone of demo)
8. `note.link` — directed link from note to any ATProto record or external URL
9. `now` — "now" page entry (append-only stream)

All lexicons authored, JSON-validated, and ready for codegen.
